FAQ

Questions, answered.

What size mug does this fit?

The packaging is designed for suitable 300–500 ml mugs. Mug dimensions and shape can vary, so unusually tall or wide mugs should be checked before ordering in bulk.

What comes with one set?

One corrugated outer box and two molded-pulp inserts. The custom label is optional.

Can I order just 1 box?

Yes. The product is available from 1 piece, with automatic bulk pricing at the listed quantity breaks.

Are labels included?

No. Labels are optional and charged separately at ₹3 each for 1–500 labels and ₹2 each from 501 labels.

Is shipping included?

No. Shipping is extra.

Can I use it for non-personalised mugs?

Yes. The packaging is suitable for appropriate 300–500 ml mugs whether or not they are personalised.
